CUSTOM SCENT PROFILES

Formulation & Scent Matching

Our formulation lab collaborates with your branding team to define your perfume profile: sweet florals, fresh citruses, deep woody notes, or traditional oriental attars. We build test compounds, check their stability in carrier solvents, adjust dry-down thresholds, and hand over a refined scent baseline that represents your brand values.

PRIMARY PACKAGING SOURCING

Custom Bottle, Collar & Cap Selection

A fragrance is recognized by its bottle design. We source premium glass bottles globally, ranging from minimalist Scandinavian cylinders to heavy-bottom luxury glass blocks, pocket spray containers, and roll-ons. Choose pump spray collar finishes (gold, silver, matte black) and custom caps to complete your brand aesthetic.

SECONDARY BRANDING

Premium Box Design & Cellowrapping

Elevate the opening experience. We guide you through cardboard paper density selection, printing techniques, rigid gift box structuring, embossing, debossing, gold foil stamping, and commercial heat-shrink cellowrapping to ensure retail shelves display a premium finished product.

LICENSING SUPPORT

Let us handle regulatory compliance and barcodes.

Launching a cosmetic product requires manufacturing licenses, barcode acquisitions, and ingredient declarations. Our administrative experts guide you through every step to ensure national compliance and international export readiness.
