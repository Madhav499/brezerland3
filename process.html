From Enquiry to Delivery: Our 5-Step Process

Getting the right fragrance solution should be simple, transparent, and stress-free. Here is exactly how we work with B2B clients — from your initial scent requirements all the way through to the final shipment arriving at your warehouse door. We provide comprehensive support at every stage of the production process. Our team not only takes pride in producing exceptional products, but also offers consultancy for clients starting their own perfume or fragrance line — from testing small quantities in the beginning, all the way to full-scale manufacturing. You just need to communicate your product specifications, fragrance positioning, quality standards, and quantity — we do the rest.

DEVELOPMENT TIMELINE

The Journey of Scent Formulation

We combine organic perfumery artistry with chemical engineering precision to manifest signature olfactory profiles for your brand.

01

Step 01: Share Your Requirements

Tell us about your product — whether it is a soap fragrance compound, a spray perfume, an attar, incense sticks, or a private-label brand you want to launch. Share the quantity, target price range, and any specific fragrance notes, references, or application requirements you have in mind. You can share your brief via WhatsApp, phone, or email. Our team will review it promptly and respond with initial guidance within 24 hours.

Contact: +91 87808 72023 | +91 9016810919 | info@breezerland.com

02

Step 02: Sample Development

Our in-house fragrance team develops a custom sample based on your brief. We focus on matching your desired scent profile while ensuring the compound is fully compatible with your intended product base — whether that is a soap, detergent, spray, oil, cream, or incense stick. Samples are carefully formulated, tested internally for stability and consistency, and then prepared for dispatch to you within 3 to 5 working days of brief confirmation.

03

Step 03: Review & Refinement

We dispatch fragrance samples for your evaluation. You share feedback — adjust the top notes, increase longevity, change the base intensity, modify the dry-down character, or request an entirely different direction. We refine iteratively until you are completely satisfied with the result. There is no rush — getting it right is more important than getting it fast.

04

Step 04: Pricing & Order Confirmation

Once your sample is approved, we provide a formal quotation with bulk pricing, minimum order quantity (MOQ), packaging options, and production and delivery timelines. You confirm the order with an advance payment, and we schedule production accordingly. All pricing is transparent — no hidden charges, no last-minute surprises.

05

Step 05: Production, Quality Check & Dispatch

We manufacture your complete order under quality-controlled, GMP-certified conditions. Before dispatch, every batch is rigorously checked for consistency, potency, stability, and safety. We then package securely and ship — with complete documentation including COA and MSDS — suited for both domestic delivery and international export.
