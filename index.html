About Breezerland: Your Trusted Fragrance Partner

Founded in 2020, Breezerland has rapidly established itself as one of Rajkot's most trusted names in fragrance compound manufacturing and export. Our Story

Breezerland was founded with a clear vision: to give businesses across India access to a reliable, quality-focused fragrance compound supplier who understands both the art of perfumery and the practicalities of B2B supply. Our founder, Parth Patadiya, combined technical expertise in fragrance chemistry with deep market knowledge to create a company that delivers real results for its clients.

Today, we serve manufacturers across personal care, household products, cosmetics, air care, and corporate gifting — providing compounds and finished fragrances that meet industry standards and consistently delight end consumers.

Fragrance Development Process

From Enquiry to Delivery: Our 5-Step Process

Getting the right fragrance solution should be simple. Here is exactly how we work with clients — from your first message to the final shipment arriving at your door.

01

Share Your Requirements

Tell us about your product — whether it's a soap fragrance compound, a spray perfume, an attar, or a private-label brand you want to launch. Share the quantity, target price range, and any specific fragrance notes or application requirements you have in mind. WhatsApp or call us at +91-8047672314 to get started.

02

Sample Development

Our in-house fragrance team develops a custom sample based on your brief. We focus on matching your desired scent profile while ensuring the compound is fully compatible with your intended product base — whether that's a soap, detergent, spray, oil, or cream.

03

Review & Refinement

We dispatch fragrance samples for your evaluation. You share feedback — adjust the top notes, increase longevity, change the base intensity, or request a completely different direction. We refine iteratively until you are completely satisfied with the result.

04

Pricing & Order Confirmation

Once your sample is approved, we provide a formal quotation with bulk pricing, minimum order quantity, packaging options, and production and delivery timelines. You confirm the order with an advance payment, and we schedule production.

05

Production, Quality Check & Dispatch

We manufacture your complete order under quality-controlled conditions. Before dispatch, every batch is checked for consistency, potency, and safety. We then package and ship securely — with documentation suited for both domestic delivery and export.
