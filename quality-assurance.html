Quality Assurance & Laboratory Controls

We ensure that every batch of fragrance compounds, attars, incense sticks, and perfumes manufactured at our Rajkot plant meets strict purity thresholds, maintains precise olfactory longevity, and carries complete regulatory documentation.

International Standards

ISO 9001:2015 & GMP Certified Quality

Our manufacturing process adheres to global standards for quality management and cosmetics manufacturing practice. Breezerland is ISO 9001:2015 and Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP) certified, providing our B2B clients with the absolute assurance that every batch is produced under secure, standardized, and cleanroom-compliant environments.

Our certifications guarantee that our raw material sourcing, formulation methods, packaging, and dispatch procedures strictly meet safety, chemical hygiene, and quality guidelines.

ANALYTICAL CHEMISTRY

GC-MS Composition Verification

Our lab uses Gas Chromatography and Mass Spectrometry (GC-MS) to verify the chemical signature of incoming botanical extracts and outgoing blends. This molecular fingerprinting guarantees that future re-orders match the approved target sample exactly, preventing batches from drifting in character.

HUMAN EXPERTISE

Olfactory Consistency Panels

While instruments verify chemical compliance, human perception defines fragrance. Our blenders conduct dry-down audits on paper strips and carrier bases over a 24-hour cycle. We assess projection intensity, dry-down balance, and raw scent retention to ensure the user gets a premium sensory experience.

STANDARDIZED QA

Our Quality Control Protocols

We execute strict inspections at three stages of our manufacturing lifecycle.

Stage 01

Raw Material Testing

We check the purity, density, refractive index, and color consistency of every incoming batch of essential oils, aroma chemicals, packaging materials, and solvents. Substandard lots are instantly rejected.

Stage 02

In-Process Inspections

During compounding, maturation, and bottling, our team monitors blending parameters (mixing speed, temperature, maturation duration) and enforces cleanroom standards for all packaging items.

Stage 03

Final Batch Audit

Prior to bulk packaging or bottling dispatch, each completed batch is checked for olfactory dry-down progression, longevity, projection performance, safety compatibility, and GC-MS comparison metrics.

B2B REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

Export-Ready Handoff Documentation

We supply complete analytical support sheets to streamline product registrations and customs clearances.

COA & MSDS Issuance

Every bulk shipment leaves our plant with a dedicated Certificate of Analysis (COA) specifying the batch density, refractive index, and a Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) detailing handling instructions.

Cosmetic Base Stability Testing

We perform compatibility testing for all fragrance compounds inside shampoos, soaps, laundry detergents, and pocket sprays to guarantee the compound doesn't cause product discoloration, separation, or base instability.
