H1 — From Enquiry to Delivery: Our 5-Step Process
Getting the right fragrance solution should be simple. Here is exactly how we work with clients —
from your first message to the final shipment arriving at your door.
STEP 01
Share Your Requirements
Tell us about your product — whether it's a soap fragrance compound, a spray
perfume, an attar, or a private-label brand you want to launch. Share the quantity,
target price range, and any specific fragrance notes, references, or application
requirements you have in mind. WhatsApp or call us at +91-8047672314 to get started.
STEP 02
Sample Development
Our in-house fragrance team develops a custom sample based on your brief. We focus
on matching your desired scent profile while ensuring the compound is fully compatible
with your intended product base — whether that's a soap, detergent, spray, oil, or
cream.
STEP 03
Review & Refinement
We dispatch fragrance samples for your evaluation. You share feedback — adjust the
top notes, increase longevity, change the base intensity, or request a completely
different direction. We refine iteratively until you are completely satisfied with the result.
STEP 04
Pricing & Order Confirmation
Once your sample is approved, we provide a formal quotation with bulk pricing,
minimum order quantity, packaging options, and production and delivery timelines. You
confirm the order with an advance payment, and we schedule production.

STEP 05
Production, Quality Check & Dispatch
We manufacture your complete order under quality-controlled conditions. Before
dispatch, every batch is checked for consistency, potency, and safety. We then
package and ship securely — with documentation suited for both domestic delivery and
export.
