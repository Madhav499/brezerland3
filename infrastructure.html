Our Manufacturing Facility & Infrastructure

Operating from our modern facility in Rajkot, Gujarat, we maintain advanced industrial blending equipment, automated packing configurations, and rigorous safety controls to support national and global B2B supply chains.

Schedule a Plant Tour Our Quality Standards
AUTOMATION CAPABILITY

High-Speed Bottling Lines

Our production floor features high-efficiency automatic and semi-automatic bottling configurations. From volumetric liquid filling to pneumatic pump crimping, collar attachment, and label applications, we achieve cleanroom bottling tolerances suitable for premium retail brands.

SCENT MATURATION

Controlled Maturation Tanks

Maturation is key to perfume depth. We utilize temperature-controlled stainless steel curing tanks where our blends mature for 2 to 4 weeks. This aging process allows top, heart, and base notes to integrate, stabilizing the olfactory profile and enhancing the perfume's skin longevity.

EQUIPMENT INVENTORY

Advanced Perfumery Machinery

Our facility is equipped with specialized hardware to guarantee batch consistency and high capacity output.

High-Precision Blending Vessels
