Our Manufacturing Services

We work with B2B clients, entrepreneurs, and global brands to deliver tailored formulations and packaging formats.

Private Label Manufacturing

We partner with brands of all sizes to create private-label perfumes, fragrance compounds, attars, incense sticks, and cosmetic products. Provide us your brand name, packaging preferences, and fragrance brief — we handle everything from formulation to finished product, ready for your shelves.

Contract Manufacturing

Breezerland provides full-service contract manufacturing for perfumes, fragrance compounds, incense sticks, and personal care products. From raw material sourcing and formulation to filling, capping, labelling, and dispatch — we manage the entire production cycle efficiently and to specification.

Custom Formulation

Our in-house perfumers specialize in custom fragrance formulations. We work closely with you to develop unique, high-quality scent profiles that align perfectly with your brand's identity and target market. From oriental attars to fresh aquatic sprays — we create fragrances your customers will love.

Perfume Brand Development & Consulting
