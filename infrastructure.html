Our Manufacturing Facility & Infrastructure

Operating from our modern facility in Rajkot, Gujarat, we maintain advanced industrial blending equipment, automated packing configurations, and rigorous safety controls to support national and global B2B supply chains.

Schedule a Plant Tour Our Quality Standards
AUTOMATION CAPABILITY

High-Speed Bottling Lines

Our production floor features high-efficiency automatic and semi-automatic bottling configurations. From volumetric liquid filling to pneumatic pump crimping, collar attachment, and label applications, we achieve cleanroom bottling tolerances suitable for premium retail brands.

SCENT MATURATION

Controlled Maturation Tanks

Maturation is key to perfume depth. We utilize temperature-controlled stainless steel curing tanks where our blends mature for 2 to 4 weeks. This aging process allows top, heart, and base notes to integrate, stabilizing the olfactory profile and enhancing the perfume's skin longevity.

EQUIPMENT INVENTORY

Advanced Perfumery Machinery

Our facility is equipped with specialized hardware to guarantee batch consistency and high capacity output.

High-Precision Blending Vessels

Specialized chemical-grade steel mixers ensuring absolute ingredient dissolution and uniform scent profile dispersion.

Automated Spray Filling Machines

Volumetric automated dispensers that fill up to 30 bottles per minute with zero leakage and perfect dosage alignment.

Curing & Aging Stainless Steel Tanks

Food-grade and cosmetics-compliant SS-316 tanks designed for scent maturation and temperature regulation.

Semi-Automatic Attar Bottling Setups

Micro-volume pneumatic injection pumps built for delicate attar and oil containers ranging from 3ml to 30ml.

LOGISTICS & RAW MATERIALS

Bulk Warehouse & Export Hub

Structured to support swift domestic transport and international oceanic cargo shipments.

Raw Oils Storage

We source raw essential oils and premium perfume concentrates globally. Our warehouse features specialized climate zoning to prevent oil oxidation and maintain ingredient potency.

Global Export Logistics

Equipped with complete documentation support including Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS), Certificate of Analysis (COA), and secure sea-freight export palletization for hassle-free custom clearances.
