About Breezerland: Your Trusted Fragrance Manufacturing Partner

Founded in 2020, Breezerland has rapidly established itself as one of Rajkot's most trusted names in fragrance compound manufacturing and export. Operating from our facility on Rajkot, Gujarat, we specialize in manufacturing fragrance compounds, perfume compounds, fine fragrances, attars, incense sticks, and a wide range of aromatic products for industrial and consumer use.

OUR LEGACY

Our Story

Breezerland was founded with a clear vision: to give businesses across India access to a reliable, quality-focused fragrance compound supplier who understands both the art of perfumery and the practicalities of B2B supply. Our founder, Parth Patadiya, combined technical expertise in fragrance chemistry with deep market knowledge to create a company that delivers real results for its clients.

Today, we serve manufacturers across personal care, household products, cosmetics, air care, incense sticks, and corporate gifting — providing compounds and finished fragrances that meet industry standards and consistently delight end consumers.

We are a pure private label and contract manufacturing facility. We do not sell products under our own consumer brand — which means your formulas, formulations, and proprietary scent profiles are completely safe with us. Over the years, we have built long-term partnerships with brands who trust our discretion, quality, and consistency.
