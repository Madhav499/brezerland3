WHO WE ARE

A Heritage of Olfactory Excellence

Breezerland is a Rajkot-based manufacturer and exporter of premium fragrance compounds, perfume compounds, fine fragrances, attars, and aromatic products. Since our establishment in 2020, we have built a strong reputation for delivering consistent, high-quality fragrance solutions tailored to the needs of cosmetic brands, personal care manufacturers, detergent producers, and corporate gifting businesses.

Whether you need a ready-to-bottle spray perfume, a custom-blended raw material compound, or end-to-end support for launching your own perfume brand — Breezerland is your one-stop fragrance manufacturing partner.
